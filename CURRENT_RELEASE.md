# Current release: v5.0.0

Release v5.0.0 extends the prospective CCA validation from the amount of payable relief to the likely timing and duration of that relief.

## Main additions

- Exact 250-word abstract and A4 portrait manuscript.
- Compensation timing measured from invoice issue to attributed operation.
- Days remaining to contractual maturity at the operation date.
- Relief generated within 7, 30, 60, and 90 days.
- Relief-days as a duration-weighted economic opportunity measure.
- Formula-variant validation for timely relief.
- Buyer-specific selection tests for thirty-day relief and relief-days.
- Expanded scientific, circularity, social, governance, and application implications.
- Primary dataset citation and DOI: https://doi.org/10.17632/28rbmvwsm9.1

## Confirmatory timing results

Across Q1 2022-Q3 2023, the seven complete quarters:

- path-attributed relief has a median 4.0-day mean issue-to-event delay, with 89.4% generated within 7 days and 94.0% within 30 days;
- cycle-attributed relief has a median 3.6-day mean delay, with 90.8% generated within 7 days and 95.9% within 30 days;
- Log-CCA has median Spearman 0.564 with integrated thirty-day relief and 0.566 with integrated relief-days;
- the highest Log-CCA quartile has 96.5% median thirty-day compensation incidence versus 57.6% in the lowest quartile;
- top Log-CCA selection captures 94.7% of buyer-specific best thirty-day relief at the median.

Conditional prediction of the exact waiting time after relief is known to occur is weaker, with median Spearman 0.209 for the integrated target. The operational claim is timely-opportunity ranking, not exact cash-date prediction.

## Preserved relief and horizon evidence

The prior prospective relief results remain unchanged. Median Log-CCA Spearman with future integrated relief is 0.638 across the seven complete quarters, and the score captures 92.5% of buyer-specific best relief at the median. Weekly-to-annual tests continue to support quarterly recalibration, monthly monitoring, and weekly diagnostics.

## Methodology

The continuous residual-state and source-fragment rules remain part of the methodology. They prevent reuse of consumed capacity and provide exact event dates for the timing analysis. They are not promoted as a hypothesis, substantive result, or conclusion.
