# Data rights and access

The MIT license in this repository applies to software only. It does not grant rights to the invoice data, manuscript, figures, or third-party materials.

The primary experimental dataset is:

> Esteva de la Rosa, Peplluis (2026), *Atomic Common-Day Invoice Clearing: Pseudonymized Invoice Records and Reproducibility Data, 2012-2023*, Mendeley Data, Version 1. https://doi.org/10.17632/28rbmvwsm9.1

Users must follow the license and access conditions stated by Mendeley Data and any additional institutional or contractual restrictions. Pseudonymized transaction networks may remain commercially sensitive because dates, amounts, concentration, and topology can facilitate re-identification.

Do not publish or commit:

- reversible company mappings, names, tax identifiers, or contact information;
- unrestricted row-level source records when redistribution is not authorized;
- source-fragment operation logs that reveal counterparties or commercially sensitive timing;
- credentials, access tokens, or private repository URLs.

The public release contains aggregate research outputs sufficient to inspect the reported results and verify the release. Exact row-level reproduction requires authorized data access.
